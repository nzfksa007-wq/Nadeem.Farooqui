Management Takeaway

Logistics performance is created at the handoffs

Reliable distribution depends on synchronizing material readiness, documentation, carrier capacity, field priorities and delivery confirmation rather than managing transport as an isolated activity.

“The shipment is only successful when every handoff is visible, owned and completed.”
