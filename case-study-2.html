Business Challenge

Planning was reacting to shortages instead of anticipating them

Demand visibility was limited, safety stock coverage was inconsistent and replenishment frequently became urgent only after material availability was already at risk. The objective was to establish a more proactive planning rhythm.

Management Takeaway

Good planning is exception driven, not shortage driven

The objective is not simply to hold more inventory. It is to identify which materials need attention, when they need it and why.

“Use demand signals to act before a stock out becomes an operational problem.”
