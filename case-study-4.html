Case Study 4: Warehouse Operations & Process Improvement

Optimizing warehouse efficiency through process redesign, automation and performance management

← Back to Portfolio

1. Define: The Business Problem

Situation

A warehouse operation was burdened by manual processes, inefficient workflows and lack of performance visibility. Order fulfillment was slow and error prone, impacting customer service.

Impact

  • Manual picking and packing processes consuming significant labor
  • High error rates in order fulfillment requiring rework
  • No real time visibility into warehouse performance and productivity
  • Inconsistent cycle times and customer delivery commitments
  • High labor costs with limited scalability

Objective

Achieve 98%+ PO fulfillment rate through process optimization, automation and real time performance management.

2. Measure: Current State Analysis

Baseline Metrics

Data Collection

  • Process time study and workflow mapping
  • Error analysis and root cause identification
  • Labor utilization and productivity assessment
  • WMS performance and system capabilities review
  • Cycle time and throughput analysis

3. Analyze: Root Cause Identification

Root Causes Identified

  • Manual Picking: Paper based picking lists and no system guidance
  • Poor Location Management: Items not in correct locations causing search time
  • No Batch Processing: Orders processed one at a time instead of batches
  • Weak QC: No systematic verification before shipment
  • Lack of Automation: No conveyor systems or automated tools

Data Analysis

Analysis showed that 60% of warehouse time was spent searching for items due to location inaccuracy and lack of system guidance. Batch processing opportunities could reduce handling by 40%.

4. Improve: Solutions & Implementation

1. Process Redesign

  • Wave based order batch processing
  • Zone picking methodology
  • Sorted order consolidation before packing
  • Standardized packing and labeling procedures

2. WMS Optimization

  • Mobile picking using barcode scanning
  • Directed picking (WMS guided putaway and picking)
  • Automated sorting and consolidation logic
  • Real time inventory visibility in WMS

3. Warehouse Layout Optimization

  • ABC location organization (fast movers near packing)
  • Standardized bin locations and organization
  • Reduced picking distance and travel time

4. Quality Control & KPI Management

  • Automated QC scanning and verification
  • Real time KPI dashboards for productivity
  • Performance metrics by zone, operator and shift

5. Control: Sustaining Results

Control Measures

  • Daily KPI Monitoring: Fulfillment rate, cycle time, error rate tracked hourly
  • Weekly Performance Reviews: Operator and zone level analysis
  • Monthly Process Audits: Adherence to procedures and continuous improvement

Sustained Results

Key Takeaways

  • PO fulfillment improved to 98%+ through process redesign and WMS optimization
  • Error rate reduced from 8-10% to <1% through automated QC and verification
  • Warehouse productivity increased 60%+ through batch processing and layout optimization
  • Labor efficiency improved with 80% reduction in manual work and system guidance
  • Real time KPI visibility enables continuous performance monitoring and improvement
