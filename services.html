Engagements can start with a focused operational review and grow only where the data and team identify a clear need. The aim is a practical next step: a control to strengthen, a process to simplify, or a KPI people can use.

Support can be scoped around warehouse operations, inventory, planning, reporting, or improvement work. The right scope depends on the process, available records, and operating priorities.

01

Warehouse operations

Review receiving, storage, picking, dispatch, location control, and material flow. Identify repeated checks and unclear handovers that slow work.

  • Process and layout review
  • Warehouse KPIs
  • Standard work and SOPs
02

Inventory accuracy & control

Build confidence in stock records through practical cycle-count routines, reconciliation, and root-cause follow-up.

  • Cycle count design
  • Stock reconciliation
  • SAP MM/WM controls
03

Availability & replenishment

Investigate stock-outs and material delays by comparing demand, lead times, stock settings, and order execution.

  • Shortage and exception analysis
  • Safety-stock review
  • Supplier and PO follow-up
04

Slow-moving stock & working capital

Segment inventory by age, movement, and value, then evaluate options for transfer, reuse, return, or other recovery paths.

  • Dead-stock analysis
  • Excess stock visibility
  • Recovery opportunity review
05

Dashboards & reporting

Make operational information easier to review with focused Excel or Power BI reporting for stock risks, movement, and service.

  • Inventory and warehouse KPIs
  • Exception reporting
  • Management dashboards
06

Process improvement & logistics

Map the flow, verify root causes, and test workable improvements across dispatch, 3PL coordination, and delivery handovers.

  • Lean Six Sigma DMAIC
  • 3PL and vendor coordination
  • Control and follow-up plans
