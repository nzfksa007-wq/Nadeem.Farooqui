My Consulting Methodology

I apply a structured Lean Six Sigma DMAIC methodology to supply chain and operational improvement. Each engagement moves from defining the business problem to achieving measurable and sustainable results.

1

Define

Understand the business problem, define objectives, establish the project scope and identify the operational requirements that matter.

2

Measure

Collect and validate operational data to establish the current state, create a reliable baseline and quantify the size of the problem.

3

Analyze

Go beyond symptoms to identify root causes using structured problem solving, data analysis and process evaluation.

4

Improve

Develop and implement practical solutions based on business impact, feasibility and the expected operational and financial benefit.

5

Control

Establish KPIs, controls, standard processes and management visibility to ensure improvements are sustained over time.

Problem  →  Data  →  Root Cause  →  Improvement  →  Sustainable Results
