Business Challenge

Inventory value was tied up in items with little or no movement

The challenge was not simply to label stock as “dead.” It was to quantify exposure, identify the materials driving it and create a practical disposition path without compromising operational requirements.

Decision Framework

Dead stock needs a disposition decision, not just a report

Age and future demand can be combined into a simple action matrix so that every flagged item moves toward a management decision.

Recovery Pipeline

Convert analysis into controlled action

The improvement process narrows the inventory population from broad screening to approved recovery or disposition actions.

1 • Identify inactive and non-moving inventory
2 • Validate demand, age and business criticality
3 • Prioritize high value exposure
4 • Recover, redeploy or dispose

Management Takeaway

Working capital improves when inventory receives a decision

The goal is not to eliminate every slow mover. It is to make inactivity visible, challenge future need and assign a controlled action to material that no longer justifies its capital exposure.
