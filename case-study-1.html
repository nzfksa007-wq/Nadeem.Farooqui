Challenge

Inventory records could not be relied on consistently

Differences between physical stock and system balances created reconciliation work and reduced confidence in material availability. The goal was not simply to correct quantities, but to build controls that prevented recurring variances.

Root Cause Analysis

Why the discrepancies were recurring

1
Transaction timing

Delayed or incorrect material movements created system differences.

2
GR / GI reconciliation

Goods receipts and issues required tighter transaction control.

3
Location accuracy

Physical storage locations did not always align with records.

4
Stock condition

Damaged or obsolete stock required timely system treatment.

5
Counting discipline

A systematic cycle count program was required.

Improvement Actions

Controls introduced to prevent recurrence

Structured cycle counting

Routine verification replaced dependence on periodic full counts.

Risk based priority

High value and higher risk inventory received greater control attention.

Variance investigation

Root causes were reviewed before inventory adjustments were made.

Transaction discipline

Goods receipt and goods issue processes were strengthened.

Location controls

Bin and storage inconsistencies were corrected.

Exception monitoring

Recurring discrepancies became visible for corrective action.
