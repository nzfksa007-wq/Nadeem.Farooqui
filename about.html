About Me

20 Years of Supply Chain Excellence in the GCC Region

My Journey

I have spent 20 years managing complex inventory operations in the GCC region. Starting with Schindler in Saudi Arabia (2006-2022) and continuing in the UAE (2022-2026), I have built a career on transforming supply chain chaos into competitive advantage.

My expertise spans 1,800+ SKU inventory management, demand forecasting, working capital optimization, and audit readiness. I have implemented systems that achieve 100% inventory accuracy, reduce stock-outs by 60-80%, and recover millions in locked capital.

Now, I am bringing 20 years of ground-truth expertise to SMEs worldwide, helping businesses transform their supply chain operations through data-driven strategy and practical execution.
