About Me

20+ years of hands on GCC experience across inventory, planning, warehouse operations, logistics and supply chain analytics.

My Journey

My career in supply chain and inventory management has been built across two major GCC markets, beginning in Saudi Arabia in 2005 and continuing through my professional journey in the United Arab Emirates from 2022 to 2026.

I began with Schindler in Saudi Arabia, developing hands on expertise in warehouse operations, spare parts, inventory control and outbound logistics. Over the years, my responsibilities expanded across material availability, transport planning, dispatch coordination, stock governance and cross functional operations.

In 2022, I continued with Schindler in the UAE in a warehouse and inventory focused role, strengthening my experience in SAP MM, WMS, KPI reporting, cycle counting, audit readiness and process improvement.

Throughout my career, I have worked with complex inventories involving more than 1,800 SKUs, technical materials and critical spare parts where stock accuracy, material availability, working capital and operational continuity directly affect business performance.

My approach combines practical operational experience with data driven decision making. Documented results used across this portfolio include achieving 100% inventory accuracy and improving the stock out rate from 2.0% to 1.06%, a 47% improvement.

Today, I am applying that experience to help SMEs and growing businesses strengthen supply chain visibility, inventory control, warehouse execution and logistics coordination through practical, measurable improvement.
