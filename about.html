About Me

Turning inventory and supply-chain complexity into measurable competitive advantage for SMEs worldwide.

Building Practical Supply Chain Advantage

I help SMEs and growing businesses turn inventory and supply-chain complexity into a measurable competitive advantage.

My career has been shaped across two major GCC markets, starting in Saudi Arabia in 200s and continuing in the UAE from 2022 to 2026. I have worked hands-on with complex inventories, technical spare parts, and high-stakes operational environments where accuracy and availability directly impact performance and profitability.

I have led warehouse operations, inventory control, 3PL logistics and aftermarket logistics for large-scale organisations. In these roles, I drove 100% inventory accuracy, reduced stock-outs by 60–80%, and helped unlock millions in tied-up working capital all while maintaining zero audit failures across multiple cycles.

Today, I bring this practical GCC experience to SMEs and growing businesses globally. My focus is not on generic process documentation, but on measurable outcomes: better stock visibility, leaner working capital, and supply chains that become a strategic edge rather than a cost centre.

I work with clients to identify where inventory and supply-chain performance is being lost, understand the root causes, and implement practical improvements that can be tracked through clear operational and financial KPIs.
