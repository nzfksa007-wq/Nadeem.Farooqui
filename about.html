About Me

Supply Chain, Inventory & Operations Expertise Across the GCC

My Journey

My career in supply chain and inventory management has been built across two major GCC markets, beginning in Saudi Arabia in 2006 and continuing through my professional journey in the United Arab Emirates from 2022 to 2026.

I began my journey with Schindler in Saudi Arabia, where I developed strong hands on expertise in warehouse operations, spare parts, inventory control, logistics, and aftermarket supply chain management. Over the years, I progressed into roles with increasing responsibility for inventory accuracy, operational efficiency, material availability, and cross functional coordination.

In 2022, I continued my journey with Schindler in the UAE, taking on a warehouse and inventory focused role where I further strengthened my expertise in compliance, stock governance, SAP MM, WMS, reporting, KPI management, and process improvement.

Throughout my career, I have worked with complex inventories involving more than 1,800 SKUs, technical materials, and critical spare parts, where inventory accuracy, material availability, working capital, and operational continuity directly impact business performance.

My approach combines practical operational experience with data driven decision making. I have consistently focused on improving inventory accuracy, reducing stock outs, optimizing working capital, strengthening audit readiness, and creating processes that provide management with greater visibility and control. My experience includes achieving 100% inventory accuracy, reducing stock outs by 60 to 80%, and identifying opportunities to unlock millions in tied up inventory capital.

Today, I am taking this practical GCC experience beyond traditional corporate roles and bringing it to SMEs and growing businesses worldwide.

My objective is simple: to help businesses transform their inventory and supply chain operations from a cost centre into a measurable competitive advantage through better processes, reliable data, stronger controls, and practical execution that delivers real business results.
